# FRAZIL Product Identity

## Product Name

**FRAZIL**

FRAZIL is the adopted working/product name for the project.

## Meaning

In physical oceanography, frazil is the collection of small ice crystals that forms in supercooled, moving or turbulent water.

## Product Metaphor

The name connects the project's two material directions:

- **Water**: flow, movement, depth and fluid texture;
- **Ice**: crystal, particulate detail, friction and cold clarity;
- **Transition**: changing states between fluid and crystalline material;
- **Sound identity**: input-driven, textural transformation rather than an unrelated one-shot sound source.

## Naming Vocabulary

These words are reserved as product, GUI or visual-language candidates. They do not change the locked parameter IDs or parameter contract:

- Flow
- Crystal

- Depth

## Naming and Legal Scope

FRAZIL is the adopted working/product name. Formal trademark or legal clearance is outside the current engineering scope.
